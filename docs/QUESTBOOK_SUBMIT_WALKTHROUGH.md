## After Submit

1. Save the Questbook proposal URL / confirmation email.  
2. Post `docs/outreach/X_POST_EN.md` on X.  
3. Post `docs/outreach/ARC_DISCORD_POST_EN.md` in Arc Discord.  
4. Send `docs/DEVELOPER_INTEGRATION_INVITE.md` to 3–5 builders.  
5. Do **not** say “Grant approved” until Circle confirms.
