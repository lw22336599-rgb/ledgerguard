# Conformance and brand-mark program

The project separates interoperability tests from certification.

## Current stage: self-test only

Anyone may run the public fixtures and report the exact implementation version,
profile version, test commit, date, and result. Passing the suite does not grant
the right to use a LedgerGuard certification mark or imply endorsement.

Allowed wording:

> Self-tested against LedgerGuard Risk Signal Profile v1 at commit `<sha>`.

## Future LedgerGuard Conformant program

The `LedgerGuard Conformant` label remains reserved until all of the following
exist:

1. a registered or otherwise enforceable brand policy reviewed by counsel;
2. a public, versioned normative profile and deterministic test suite;
3. an independent runner and tamper-evident result record;
4. issuance, expiration, suspension, appeal, and revocation rules;
5. conflict-of-interest separation between paid commercial work and scoring;
6. published compatibility and security limitations;
7. at least three independent integrations and one non-LedgerGuard
   implementation of the profile.

Until these gates pass, the hosted service must not display `Certified`,
`LedgerGuard Conformant`, or language implying official x402 approval.

## Why the mark is not the only moat

A brand label without adoption and enforcement is easy to imitate. Defensibility
must combine maintained threat data, reliable evidence, distribution inside
wallets and agent workflows, measurable evaluation quality, and operational
service guarantees.
